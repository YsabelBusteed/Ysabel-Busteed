The Drop

Galeries Lafayette

Ads

My own concept for a quick 30% off sale on the new season collection. I named the campaign, designed the logo and built the story ads.

Why The Drop: new season pieces had just dropped, and so had the prices.

Every visual drops you into an adrenaline rush: skydiving, sandboarding down the dunes, speeding along the Palm. The lettering warps as if caught mid-fall, so a short sale feels urgent and exciting.

  • ClientGaleries Lafayette Dubai
  • RoleConcept, naming, logo design, ads
  • Offer30% off select new season
